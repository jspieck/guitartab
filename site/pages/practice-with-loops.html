Practise with loops and a speed trainer

The fastest way to learn a hard passage: play it slowly, over and over, and speed up a little each time. SereneTab does the looping and the counting for you, with any tab you open.

Loop a passage

  • Select the bars: Shift+click in the tab, or drag over the bar numbers in the sequencer. Playback repeats them.
  • Songs with markers (Intro, Verse, Solo…) show them in the sequencer: click one to loop that section.

Slow down, then speed up

  • Choose a speed from 25 % to 150 % in the toolbar; the pitch stays the same.
  • Switch on the speed trainer: after every pass of the loop it adds 2, 5 or 10 % until it reaches the speed you set.
  • SereneTab remembers the speed and loop for each song, so you continue where you stopped.

Keep time

  • A count-in bar before playing and a metronome that follows tempo changes.
  • A big beat counter you can read from a few metres away.
  • Mute your own part in the sequencer and play along with the rest of the band.

And a tuner

The tuner listens through your microphone, only while its window is open. The sound is analysed on your device and never sent anywhere.
