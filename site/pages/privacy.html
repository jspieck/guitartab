Privacy in short

SereneTab runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, no cookies, no tracking and no content from third parties. Your songs and files do not leave your device.

This is an English summary. The binding privacy policy is the German Datenschutzerklärung; the operator is named in the Impressum.

What the server sees

The site is hosted by netcup GmbH in Germany. Like every web server, it writes a log of each request (IP address, time, file, size, status, referrer and browser name) to deliver the site and keep it secure. The logs are deleted after a limited period (see the German policy).

What stays in your browser

Your settings, the automatic backups of the song you are editing and your practice settings (speed, loop) are stored in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Files, microphone and MIDI

Files you open are read in your browser and not uploaded; exports are created in your browser and saved directly to your device. The tuner uses the microphone only after you allow it and only while it is open; the sound is analysed locally. MIDI devices are likewise used only locally after you allow it.

No third parties

Fonts, sounds and example songs come from this server. There is no analytics, no advertising, no social media plugin and no content delivery network.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of your data, to data portability and to object; you can also complain to a data protection authority. The Datenschutzerklärung has the details and the contact address.
