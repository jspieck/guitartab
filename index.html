SereneTab

A free guitar tab editor and player in your browser. Open Guitar Pro, MusicXML and MIDI files, listen to every track, practise with loops and a speed trainer, and write your own tabs, on a computer or a phone. No account, nothing to install; your songs stay on your device.

Loading the editor

  • Opens Guitar Pro 3–8 (.gp, .gpx, .gp5, .gp4, .gp3), MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl), MIDI and .gt files
  • Saves as Guitar Pro 5, Guitar Pro 6–8, MusicXML, MIDI, PDF and .gt
  • Tab with classical notation, a score view for several tracks, drums, lyrics, two voices
  • Playback with real instrument sounds, mute and solo, loops, speed trainer, count-in, metronome, tuner
  • A phone layout with a touch fret pad, pinch to zoom and swipe between beats
