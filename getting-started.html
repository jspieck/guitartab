Getting started

SereneTab is a guitar tab editor and player that runs in your browser. You need no account and nothing to install, and your songs stay on your device. This page shows the first steps and lists every keyboard shortcut.

1. Enter notes

  • Click a line of the tab to put the cursor on that string, then type a fret number. Type two digits quickly for frets 10 and up.
  • Move with the arrow keys: ← → between beats, ↑ ↓ between strings.
  • Pick the length of the next note with W H Q E S T (whole to thirty-second) or the toolbar; . dots it, Alt+3 makes a triplet.
  • Effects (bends, slides, hammer-ons, vibrato, palm mutes…) are in the Effects tab of the toolbar, or in the note menu: right-click a note or press Shift+F10.
  • Everything can be undone with Ctrl/⌘+Z.

On a phone, tap the pencil, then a string in the tab, then a fret on the pad. See tabs on your phone.

2. Listen

Press Space or the play button. Playback starts at the cursor. The first time, the instrument sounds take a moment to load. Each track has its own volume, mute and solo in the sequencer; the guitar and piano windows show the notes as they play.

3. Open your own tabs

The folder button opens Guitar Pro (.gp, .gpx, .gp5, .gp4, .gp3), MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl), MIDI and SereneTab's own .gt files. Nothing is uploaded. Save your work as .gt, or export Guitar Pro, MusicXML, MIDI or PDF. More on opening Guitar Pro files.

Your current song is also saved automatically in the browser. If something goes wrong, File → Recover drafts brings earlier versions back.

4. Practise

Select a few bars (Shift+click, or drag over the bar numbers in the sequencer) to loop them, slow them down with the speed menu, and switch on the speed trainer to get faster with every pass. A count-in, metronome, big beat counter and tuner are there too. More on practising with loops.

Keyboard shortcuts

The same list is in the app: press ?. Ctrl/⌘ means Ctrl on Windows and Linux, ⌘ on a Mac.

Enter notes

0–9Enter a fret on the selected string (type two digits quickly for 10 and up); on drum tracks a General MIDI drum number (36 kick)
Delete or BackspaceDelete the selected note, or every note in the selected range
EscClear the selection

Drum tracks

KKick on / off at the cursor
SSnare on / off at the cursor
XSide stick on / off at the cursor
HHi-hat on / off at the cursor
OOpen hi-hat on / off at the cursor
PPedal hi-hat on / off at the cursor
CCrash on / off at the cursor
IRide on / off at the cursor
BRide bell on / off at the cursor
UHigh tom on / off at the cursor
MMid tom on / off at the cursor
FFloor tom on / off at the cursor

Move

←Previous beat
→Next beat
↑Next higher string
↓Next lower string
Alt+1Edit voice 1
Alt+2Edit voice 2 (drawn in its own colour)
Alt+↑Previous track (in the score view: the track above)
Alt+↓Next track (in the score view: the track below)

Select

Shift+←Extend the selection a beat left (also Shift+click)
Shift+→Extend the selection a beat right
Ctrl/⌘+ASelect the bar; again: the whole track
Ctrl/⌘+↑Transpose the selection a semitone up
Ctrl/⌘+↓Transpose the selection a semitone down
Ctrl/⌘+Shift+↑Transpose the selection an octave up
Ctrl/⌘+Shift+↓Transpose the selection an octave down

Note length

WWhole note
H or Shift+HHalf note (Shift+H on drum tracks)
QQuarter note
EEighth note
S or Shift+SSixteenth note (Shift+S on drum tracks)
TThirty-second note

Rhythm

.Dotted note on / off
Alt+3Triplet (Alt+5, 6, 7, 9: other tuplets); again: off
RMake the beat a rest
LTie to the previous note on the string
InsertInsert a rest here (current note length); the bar moves right
Ctrl/⌘+Delete or Ctrl/⌘+BackspaceDelete the beat; the bar moves left

Playback

SpacePlay / pause

Edit

Ctrl/⌘+ZUndo
Ctrl/⌘+Y or Ctrl/⌘+Shift+ZRedo
Ctrl/⌘+XCut the selection (or the beat)
Ctrl/⌘+CCopy the selection (or the beat), with effects and lyrics
Ctrl/⌘+VPaste over the selection, or from the cursor on
Ctrl/⌘+Shift+VPaste as new bars before the cursor’s bar
Shift+F10 or MenuNote menu at the cursor (effects, dynamics, length, bar, text); also a right-click
Shift+LLyrics at the cursor: Space or Tab next note, - next syllable, ↑/↓ verse, Enter done

View

Ctrl/⌘+WheelZoom the tab (mouse wheel)
?Show this list
